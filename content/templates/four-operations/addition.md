# Addition

## Description
**One number**, has a **second number** ***added***, which ***equals*** a **third number**.
e.g. 
	3 + 5 = 8
Further numbers can be ***added***, to further modify the **total**.
e.g.
	3 + 5 + 2 = 10
- This can be broken down into:
	3 + 5 = 8
	*then...*
	8 + 2 = 10
- *or:*
	( 3 + 5 ) + 2 = 10
This addition can also be expressed as the ***sum*** of multiple numbers.
e.g.
	The sum of 3, 5, and 2 is 10.

## Key Signs and Symbols

### + - Plus sign

## Key Words and Terms 

### Add 
*verb*
- Numbers are added together to create a total.
### Addition
*noun*
- The act of adding numbers together.
