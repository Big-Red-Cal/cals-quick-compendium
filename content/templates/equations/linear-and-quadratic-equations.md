# Linear & Quadratic Equations

## Linear Equations

An equation that may be put in the form:
### $a_1x_1 + ... a_nx_n + b = 0$


where x1,…,xn![{\displaystyle x_{1},\ldots ,x_{n}}](https://wikimedia.org/api/rest_v1/media/math/render/svg/737e02a5fbf8bc31d443c91025339f9fd1de1065) are the [variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variable_\(mathematics\) "Variable (mathematics)") (or [unknowns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unknown_\(mathematics\) "Unknown (mathematics)")), and b,a1,…,an![{\displaystyle b,a_{1},\ldots ,a_{n}}](https://wikimedia.org/api/rest_v1/media/math/render/svg/55ac23436a15ffd304f7e517bf39be74349a7d77) are the [coefficients](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coefficient "Coefficient"), which are often [real numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_number "Real number").
