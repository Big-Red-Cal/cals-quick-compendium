# Bearings

## Outline

### Description

- Bearings are simply a direction calculated from a fixed reference point.

#### Where do we use them?

- Bearings are commonly used on maps and technical drawings and in programming graphics.
	- Maps are used to navigate when orienteering.
	- Technical drawings are used in Construction and Engineering when designing buildings and products.
		- These drawings are then used by builders, engineers, and various other workers to guide their work.
		- They could also be used in automated machinery

### Direction

- Numerically, the bearing is an **angle** that represents the **amount of rotation** from the reference point to the target point.
	- We typically use **degrees** to measure our angles.
	- We measure bearings clockwise from the reference point.
		- (A negative direction would represent an anti-clockwise movement.)

### Reference Point

- The reference point is commonly referred to as the **North Point**.
