# Summary of Resources
This page is a temporary dump of my known resources.

This list will be expanded and refined so make sure to check back in to see what's new or different.

## Websites

### Education and Learning

#### [BBC Bitesize](https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize)

#### [Corbett Maths](https://corbettmaths.com/)
- Videos and practice questions on the GCSE maths curriculum.
	- Videos typically 5 - 10 minutes, if not shorter.
	- Practice questions come with detailed answer videos.
		- These videos are longer but very detailed.
- Sometimes provides predictions for upcoming GCSE topics.
- Also sells books and resources.

#### [Mr Barton Maths](https://www.mrbartonmaths.com/)
- A large variety of resources from Craig Barton, as well as links to further resources.
- [Standards Units Rich Tasks](https://mrbartonmaths.com/rich/standards-units.html)
	- Encourage active learning.
	- Recommended by NCETM

#### HegartyMaths / Sparx Maths
- Recommended by Craig Barton
	- Colin Hegarty is Sparx Learning's CEO
	- Oxygen House is the principal shareholder
- [HEGARTYMATHS - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@HEGARTYMATHS)

#### Peter Mattock's Websites
- [YouTube - Peter Mattock](https://www.youtube.com/c/PeterMattock) - Videos about using manipulatives to teach maths.
- [Frayer-Model.co.uk](https://www.frayer-model.co.uk/) - Vocabulary definitions, characteristics, examples, and non-examples.
- [more-same-less.co.uk](https://www.more-same-less.co.uk/) - Grid activity involving manipulating an initial value.
- [Front-Loaded Feedback](https://www.front-loaded-feedback.co.uk/) Questions with front-loaded feedback, to avoid common misconceptions.
- [Goal Free Problems](https://goalfreeproblems.blogspot.com/) Questions with specific goals removed. Learners calculate what they can.

#### [Maths Genie](https://www.mathsgenie.co.uk/) 
- Video walkthroughs, write-ups, practice and other resources.
	- [GCSE Revision](https://www.mathsgenie.co.uk/gcse.php)
		- Videos, questions, and solutions.
	- [Scheme of Work](https://www.mathsgenie.co.uk/scheme.php)
		- A scheme of work to learn maths from first steps to higher GCSE.
	- [Starter Activities](https://www.mathsgenie.co.uk/starters.php)
		- Excel based question generators and times table practice.
	- [Mini Tests](https://www.mathsgenie.co.uk/targettests.php)
		- One page tests at various GCSE levels.
	- [Edexcel GCSE Maths - 45 Minute Tests](https://www.mathsgenie.co.uk/practicetests.php)
		- Past papers split into 45 minute tests.
		- Questions do not seem to have been reshuffled.
	- [GCSE Predicted Topics](https://www.mathsgenie.co.uk/predictedpapers.php)
	- [Other Resources](https://www.mathsgenie.co.uk/teachingresources.php)

#### [Third Space Learning](https://thirdspacelearning.com/)
- An AI maths tutor (no quantifiable claims on effectiveness), but also a selection of free resources.
	- As these resources are essentially marketing for their primary service, extra scrutiny is advised.
		- [Secondary Resources](https://thirdspacelearning.com/secondary-resources/)
		- [GCSE Maths Revision Guides](https://thirdspacelearning.com/gcse-maths/revision-guides/)
		- [GCSE Maths Papers & Past Papers](https://thirdspacelearning.com/secondary-resources/gcse-maths-past-papers/)
		- [GCSE Maths Revision](https://thirdspacelearning.com/secondary-resources/gcse-maths/)

#### [JustMaths - Maths Tutorials, Resources and Support](https://justmaths.co.uk/)

#### [MathsThroughStories.org - HOME](https://www.mathsthroughstories.org/)

#### [WWW.M4THS.COM](https://m4ths.com/index.html)
